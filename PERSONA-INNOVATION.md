# Persona Design Evolution for Partnership Network Intelligence Systems

**Date:** June 17, 2026  
**Discovery:** John Michael & Otis  
**Innovation:** Adapting persona design principles for collaborative intelligence platforms

---

## Traditional Persona Design Limitations

### **Single-Purpose Tool Assumption:**
- **One user type** → **One primary use case** → **One success metric**
- **Fixed roles** - "Marketing Manager uses marketing tools"
- **Individual-focused** - person using tool alone
- **Static needs** - requirements don't change based on collaboration context

### **Why This Breaks Down for PNI Systems:**

**Partnership Network Intelligence enables:**
✅ **Multi-capability platforms** serving diverse collaborative needs  
✅ **Role fluidity** - same person may lead strategy or execute content  
✅ **Dynamic specialization** - AI network adapts to collaboration context  
✅ **Compound value creation** - outcomes impossible for individual use  

---

## Partnership Persona Framework

### **Core Principles Retained:**
- **User empathy** - understand actual needs and contexts
- **Success criteria** - measurable outcomes that matter  
- **Pain point identification** - what doesn't work today
- **Behavioral insight** - how people actually work vs. how they say they work

### **Framework Evolution:**

## **Collaboration Context Personas**

Instead of "Who uses it?" ask **"What collaboration does it enable?"**

### **Context 1: Strategic Campaign Development**
**Human Lead:** Content Strategy Lead  
**Human Support:** Product Marketing, Design, Research  
**AI Network:** Maya (Strategy), Devon (Research), Priya (Distribution), Content AI  
**Collaboration Pattern:** Strategic direction → Research validation → Content creation → Distribution optimization  
**Success:** Coherent multi-touchpoint campaign with accelerated development time

### **Context 2: Cross-Functional Product Launch** 
**Human Lead:** Product Manager  
**Human Support:** Marketing, UX, Engineering, Sales teams  
**AI Network:** Strategy, Technical, Market Analysis, Communications specialists  
**Collaboration Pattern:** Requirements → Design → Development → Go-to-market coordination  
**Success:** Aligned cross-functional execution with reduced coordination overhead

### **Context 3: Executive Decision Support**
**Human Lead:** C-Suite Executive  
**Human Support:** Strategy, Operations, Finance teams  
**AI Network:** Analysis, Research, Modeling, Presentation specialists  
**Collaboration Pattern:** Problem definition → Data gathering → Analysis → Stakeholder communication  
**Success:** Well-informed decisions with organizational buy-in

---

## **Key Innovation: "Context-Adaptive Personas"**

### **Individual Contributor Context:**
**Same person** may need different AI network configurations:
- **As Individual:** Content creation with AI writing partner
- **As Team Lead:** Campaign coordination with multi-AI network  
- **As Stakeholder:** Decision support with analysis AI network
- **As Collaborator:** Cross-functional project with specialized AI roles

### **Dynamic Role Recognition:**
**System adapts AI network based on:**
- **Current project phase** (research vs. execution vs. optimization)
- **Collaboration context** (individual work vs. team coordination vs. stakeholder presentation)
- **Expertise needs** (technical depth vs. strategic breadth vs. creative ideation)
- **Timeline pressure** (rapid iteration vs. comprehensive analysis)
